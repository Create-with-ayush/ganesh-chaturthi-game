# GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD)
## MUSHAK – BAPPA'S FESTIVAL QUEST

### 1. High Concept & Vision
"MUSHAK – BAPPA'S FESTIVAL QUEST" is a vibrant, celebratory 2D browser adventure celebrating Ganesh Chaturthi. The player assumes the role of **Mushak**, the loyal mouse vahana of Lord Ganesha. With the arrival of Ganpati Bappa imminent, Mushak undertakes five joyful challenges to collect prasad, build the grand pandal, compose sacred rangoli mandalas, lead the festival dhol drums, and adopt eco-conscious festival traditions.

### 2. Core Pillars
1. **Cultural Reverence & Authenticity**: Lord Ganesha is strictly honored as a mentor, guiding divinity, and festival centerpiece. No violence, injury, or disrespect.
2. **Accessible, Varied Indie Mini-Games**: Blends platforming, spatial puzzle design, memory matching, and rhythm gameplay into a cohesive festival narrative.
3. **Eco-Friendly Devotion**: Champions sustainable practices (natural clay idols, fresh flower garlands, biodegradable food dyes, home water immersion).
4. **Zero-Dependency Robustness**: Fully self-contained procedural audio and vector rendering guaranteeing 100% offline uptime and cross-device compatibility.

### 3. Visual & Aesthetic Identity
- **Palette**: Warm cream (`#FFFDF6`), Charcoal (`#211615`), Sindoor red (`#D62828`), Saffron (`#F77F00`), Haldi gold (`#FFB703`), Leaf green (`#2D6A4F`).
- **Motifs**: Sacred Kalash, modaks with saffron threads, hanging marigold torans, brass temple bells, flickering earthen diyas.
- **Character Design**:
  - *Mushak*: Slate velvet coat, expressive warm eyes, festive saffron/red ribbon dhoti, forehead tilak, dynamic animated poses.
  - *Lord Ganesha*: Seated in Lalitasana on a blooming padmasana lotus throne, crown adorned with gems, Abhaya Mudra, glowing Prabhavali halo.

### 4. Audio Direction
- Native Web Audio API procedural synthesis.
- Authentic Dhol bol synthesis:
  - `DHA`: Pitch-envelope bass drop with punch.
  - `DHI`: Mid-frequency slap with tuned resonance.
  - `TA`: High-frequency rimshot.
  - `NA`: Open ringing skin overtone.
- Melodic Raag Bhupali festive phrases played on flute/sitar harmonic oscillators alongside an ambient Tanpura drone.
- Authentic sound effects: Shankh horn blast, temple bell (*ghanti*) ring, modak collection chimes, and powder puff swooshes.
