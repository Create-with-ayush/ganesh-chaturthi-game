# CONTROLS & ERGONOMICS GUIDE
## MUSHAK – BAPPA'S FESTIVAL QUEST

### Desktop & Laptop
- **Level 1: Modak Rush**
  - `A` / `D` or `Left` / `Right` Arrow: Move Mushak across festival street
  - `Space` or `W` or `Up` Arrow: Jump over festival obstacles / reach high modaks
- **Level 2: Pandal Decorator**
  - `Mouse Drag & Drop`: Drag ornaments from bottom inventory tray onto target outline slots
  - `Mouse Click to Select`: Click item in tray, then click target slot to place
- **Level 3: Rangoli Memory**
  - `Mouse Click`: Select color powder pot at bottom tray
  - `Mouse Click on Petal`: Fill selected rangoli petal/dot with the active color
- **Level 4: Dhol Rhythm**
  - `A` Key: Strike **DHA** (Lane 1 - Bass Beat)
  - `S` Key: Strike **DHI** (Lane 2 - Mid Slap)
  - `D` Key: Strike **TA** (Lane 3 - Rimshot)
  - `F` Key: Strike **NA** (Lane 4 - Open Ring)
- **Bonus Level: Eco-Friendly Festival**
  - `Mouse Click`: Click left or right card to select the sustainable festival choice
- **Puja Mode: Shri Ganesh Aarti & Shankh Ceremony**
  - `Mouse Drag / Touch Drag`: Swirl the sacred Karpura Aarti Thali clockwise around Lord Ganesha to complete *Pradakshina*
  - `🔔 RING BELL`: Ring continuous harmonic temple brass bells
  - `🪘 BLOW SHANKH`: Blow the sacred Conch horn with expanding golden shockwaves
  - `🌸 OFFER FLOWERS`: Scatter marigolds and holy turmeric & vermilion Akshata rice
- **Creative Features**
  - **Prasad Surge (Level 1)**: Fills with collected modaks; activates golden aura, Modak Magnet, and transforms obstacles into flower petals!
  - **Jugalbandi Fever (Level 4)**: High-speed rhythm fever with double score multiplier and crowd *"Moriya!"* cheers.
  - **Festival Wardrobe**: Equip unlocked outfits (Puneri Pagdi, Mini Dholak, Golden Ghungroos with musical footsteps, Royal Pitambara).
  - **Blessing Card Studio**: Personalize and download a custom gold-bordered Ganesh Chaturthi greeting card (PNG).
- **Global Shortcuts**
  - `Escape`: Toggle Pause Menu during active gameplay

### Mobile & Touch Devices
- **Level 1**: On-screen `◀` and `▶` touch buttons + large `JUMP` button
- **Level 2**: Touch item in tray, then touch pandal slot (or drag directly)
- **Level 3**: Tap powder pot, then tap rangoli segment
- **Level 4**: 4 large colorful drum pads (`DHA`, `DHI`, `TA`, `NA`) at bottom of screen
- **Bonus Level**: Tap card choice directly
- **Aarti Ceremony**: Swirl thali with finger touch; tap on-screen Bell, Shankh, and Flower buttons.
