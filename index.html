🎮 HOW TO PLAY

Master all festive challenges to complete Bappa's quest!

🥟
Level 1: Modak Rush
Desktop: Arrow Keys / WASD to move, Space to Jump.
Mobile: Use on-screen Left/Right and Jump buttons.
Collect Golden Modaks (+100) and Special Modaks (+250). Chain modaks for up to x5 Combos! ⚠️ Hitting any obstacle = Game Over! Fill the Prasad Meter to activate Surge mode and purify obstacles!
🏛️
Level 2: Pandal Decorator
Drag and drop or tap items from the bottom inventory into their correct sacred target slots on the Grand Pandal. Fast placements yield bonus points!
🌺
Level 3: Rangoli Memory
Memorize the sacred rangoli pattern in 3.5 seconds. Once hidden, select colored powders from the tray and tap the petals to recreate the mandala accurately!
🥁
Level 4: Dhol Rhythm
Desktop: Keys A, S, D, F for DHA, DHI, TA, NA.
Mobile: Tap the colored drum pads at the bottom.
Hit notes right on the target line for PERFECT timing (+150) and build the FESTIVAL COMBO!
🌱
Bonus: Eco-Friendly Festival
Choose environmentally responsible festival practices (natural clay murtis, fresh marigolds, organic colors, home visarjan) to protect Mother Earth and earn up to +1000 bonus points!
